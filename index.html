Ask Questions
What services does PCD provide?

PCD provides Civil & Structural Engineering, Civil Design Assurance, Temporary Works and Protection & Control support for major power and infrastructure projects.

What types of projects does PCD support?

PCD works on major power and infrastructure projects, including substation programmes. Project experience includes 400 kV substations, a 400 kV GIS substation and 400/132 kV substation civil and structural engineering.

Who does PCD work with?

PCD works both as a specialist subcontractor and directly with project owners. Partners include main contractors, construction companies, infrastructure companies, engineering companies and project owners.

Where does PCD work?

PCD supports projects across the UK. Selected projects are located in Cornwall, Essex, Lincolnshire, Hertfordshire, and London and Southeast England.

How does the design assurance process work?

The route runs in four stages: Receive, where the design package is submitted; Assure, a technical review with site context in view; Resolve, where comments and revisions are tracked; and Approve, progressing to client sign-off.

How do I start a project enquiry?

Use the enquiry form on the Contact page, or email hr@civildesignltd.com. You can also call +44 7391544743 or +44 7759170640.
